Five-day program

Your Salalah itinerary

Day 1 — UAE to Salalah

Pickup from your confirmed UAE location, UAE exit and Oman entry formalities with tour-team guidance, refreshment stop, dinner stop in Ibri at your own expense, then overnight journey by luxury bus.

Day 2 — Arrival & West Coast

Arrive in Salalah, hotel check-in and buffet lunch. Visit Mughsail Beach, Marneef Cave and seasonal blowholes, Cheraman Perumal Tomb and Souq Al Haffa. Buffet dinner and hotel night.

Day 3 — Nature & Mountains

Breakfast, Wadi Darbat, Jabal Samhan Viewpoint, packed lunch, Location of Gravity, Ayn Razat and Ayn Sahalnoot. Return for buffet dinner and second hotel night.

Day 4 — Culture, Market & Departure

Breakfast and check-out, Nabi Ayub (Alayhi as-Salam) Tomb, traditional fruit and vegetable market, buffet lunch, then depart for Dubai. Dinner stop at Haima Restaurant at your own expense.

Day 5 — Return to UAE

Complete the overnight return journey. Expected Dubai arrival is approximately 06:00 AM, depending on traffic, border formalities and road conditions.
